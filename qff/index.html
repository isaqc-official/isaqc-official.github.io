What is Qiskit Fall Fest?

A quantum computing celebration featuring workshops, hackathons, mini-challenges, games, and expert panels - all part of IIIT's Infinium tech fest.

3 Hands-on Workshops

Deep-dive into quantum computing fundamentals, quantum algorithms, and practical Qiskit programming through guided workshops.

3 Mini-Challenges

Quick problem-solving challenges to test your quantum knowledge and win exciting prizes throughout the event.

Interactive Mini-Games

Fun quantum-themed games and activities to learn concepts while having fun during breaks and networking sessions.

Hackathon with 4 Problem Statements

Team up and tackle real-world quantum computing challenges across four different problem domains and compete for prizes.

Expert Panel Discussion

Engage with industry experts and quantum researchers in insightful discussions about the future of quantum computing.

Global Quantum Community

Connect with quantum enthusiasts from around the world and build lasting relationships in the quantum computing ecosystem.
